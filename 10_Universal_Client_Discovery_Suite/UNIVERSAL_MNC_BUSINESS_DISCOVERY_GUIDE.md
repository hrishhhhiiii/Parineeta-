## 🚀 How to Use & Share with Clients

### Option A: Interactive Web Scoping Portal (Recommended for High-Touch Clients)
Open or send [`universal_discovery_portal.html`](file:///c:/Users/hrish/Desktop/logo/universal_discovery_portal.html):
- **Industry Selector:** The client selects their specific vertical (E-Commerce, SaaS, B2B, Healthcare, etc.).

- **Dark / Light Mode:** Built-in theme switch.
- **One-Click Export:** Client can export their filled responses as a formatted `.json` scoping document or copy the executive summary directly to clipboard.
- **Local Persistence:** Form draft is automatically saved to browser storage so progress is never lost.

## 🏛 The 7 Universal Pillars of Discovery

1. **Pillar 1: Enterprise Overview & Commercial Model**
   - Legal corporate structure, key decision-maker designation.
   - Core commercial model: B2C (D2C), B2B, Hybrid, Marketplace, Subscription, or Service billing.
   - Lifecycle stage: Early-stage startup vs. offline brand transitioning to digital vs. enterprise replatforming.

2. **Pillar 2: Products, Services & Value Proposition**
   - Core value proposition & competitive advantage.
   - Catalog size & SKU volume at launch (1–25, 26–100, 101–500, 500+).
   - Customization degree: Standard/off-the-shelf vs. configurable vs. bespoke.

3. **Pillar 3: Target Audience, Personas & Market Reach**
   - Buyer persona breakdown (B2B corporate roles vs. consumer demographics).
   - Geographic target: Local metro, National, or Global cross-border.
   - Expected Average Order Value (AOV) / Deal Size tiers.

4. **Pillar 4: Digital Solution Architecture & CX Features**
   - Vehicle type: E-Commerce Store, Corporate Lead Engine, Member/Booking Portal, Custom Web App/SaaS, or Mobile App.
   - Functional capabilities: Payments, multi-currency, booking calendars, WhatsApp CRM, customer accounts, social proof walls.
   - CMS preference: Shopify Plus, WooCommerce, Webflow, Custom Next.js + Node.js, or agency recommendation.

5. **Pillar 5: Operational Workflows, Payments & Integrations**
   - Payment instruments: Cards, UPI, Netbanking, International Stripe/PayPal, BNPL/EMI, subscriptions, COD, B2B purchase orders.
   - Integrations: CRM (HubSpot/Salesforce), ERP, Email automation (Klaviyo), Courier APIs (Shiprocket/DHL), Accounting (Zoho/QuickBooks).

6. **Pillar 6: Brand Assets, Content & Creative Readiness**
   - Brand guidelines readiness (vector logos, design systems).
   - Copywriting & media readiness (photos, videos, copy).
   - Reference benchmark websites admired.

7. **Pillar 7: Project Governance, Budget & Launch Milestones**
   - Delivery horizon: Accelerated sprint (3–4 weeks), standard (6–8 weeks), enterprise phased (10–14 weeks).
   - Budget allocation tiers: Essential, Professional, Enterprise.
   - Post-launch retainers: Cloud maintenance, SEO, performance marketing.
