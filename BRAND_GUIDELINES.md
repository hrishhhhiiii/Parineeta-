## 1. Brand Story & Cultural Philosophy

**পরিণীতা (Parineeta)** signifies the eternal grace, beauty, and auspicious beginnings of a Bengali bride. The brand celebrates the timeless rituals of traditional Bengali weddings, reimagined into a luxury contemporary brand aesthetic.

### The Sacred Wedding Motifs

1. **শোলার মুকুট (Bridal Shola Mukut):** Handcrafted from pristine white *sholapith* (cork reed). Worn by the bride as the living embodiment of Devi Lakshmi.
2. **শোলার টোপর (Groom Shola Topor):** The sacred conical crown worn by the groom, carrying divine blessings for marital prosperity and status.
3. **পান পাতা ও চন্দন কল্কা (Paan Pata & Chandan Kalka):** The golden betel leaf adorned with white chandan paste, commemorating the sacred *'Shubho Drishti'* (the auspicious first glance).
4. **শাঁখা ও পলা (Shankha & Pola):** The consecrated conch-shell (white) and red coral bangles that symbolize marital protection, fidelity, and happiness.
5. **আলপনা ও পদ্ম (Alpana & Lotus):** Sacred rice-paste filigree artwork welcoming abundance into the home.

## 4. Typography Guidelines

### Primary Bengali Script
- **Display / Title:** `Tiro Bangla` or `Noto Serif Bengali` (Weight: Bold / 700)
- **Calligraphic Wedding:** `Galada` or custom vector calligraphy
- **Tagline:** `Noto Serif Bengali` (Weight: Semi-Bold / 600) with generous tracking (letter spacing)

### Complementary Latin / English Script
- **Luxury Serif:** `Cinzel` or `Cormorant Garamond` (Weights: 600, 700)
- **Modern Clean UI:** `Plus Jakarta Sans` or `Montserrat` (Weights: 400, 500)

---

## 5. Clear Space & Size Guidelines

- **Clear Space:** Maintain minimum clear space equal to the height of the letter **"প"** around the entire logo to ensure visibility and prestige.
- **Minimum Digital Size:** Primary Crest: 120px × 120px; Wordmark: 180px wide; Favicon: 32px × 32px.
- **Minimum Print Size:** Primary Crest: 25mm diameter; Wordmark: 40mm wide.
