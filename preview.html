Authentic Bengali Wedding Symbolism

👑 Shola Mukut & Topor

Handcrafted from pure white sholapith (Indian cork). The Mukut crowns the bride like Devi Lakshmi, while the Topor crowns the groom like Lord Shiva.

🍃 Paan Pata (Betel Leaf)

Central to 'Shubho Drishti' (the auspicious first glance), where the bride holds betel leaves in front of her eyes before revealing her face to the groom.

🐚 Shankha & Pola

The timeless wedding bangles: the white Shankha carved from conch shells and red Pola from red coral, signifying good health, love, and marital prosperity.

✨ Alpana & Chandan Kalka

Sacred ritual folk art painted with rice flour and chandan paste on the floor, wedding mandap, and the bride's forehead for divine blessing.
